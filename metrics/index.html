Everyday Dose

Brand and messaging across two chapters of a fast-growing company.

  • Brand Strategy
  • Content Strategy
  • Copywriting

2021 to 2023: Building the foundations.

I joined in September 2021, the company's first year. With the founder and core team, I established the brand and content guidelines and a science-first voice that kept the brand fun and accessible. I led copywriting for the website, social media, products and merchandise.

2025: Unifying brand and messaging across touchpoints.

I came back in May 2025 as Messaging Lead and Brand Strategist during the company's extension from D2C into retail. Working closely with the executive team and Brand Strategy Lead, I wrote and owned the brand positioning and messaging framework to bring coherence across teams and touchpoints. I owned messaging across website, social, product, packaging, and events; wrote briefs for UGC creators, and supported the launch into 1,500 Target stores across the country, including an out-of-home campaign in NYC spanning Penn Station, taxis, and street side kiosks.

              How I work

              Brand strategy decides what an organization stands for and why anyone should care. Content and copy decide how the story gets told, and the exact words.

              Brand Strategy

              Identity

              What the organization stands for.

              Purpose, vision, mission, values.

              Interviews and research with the team, turned into brand and content strategy (Synthesis Institute).

              Positioning

              Where it belongs, and why someone would choose it.

              Market research, competitive analysis, differentiators, a positioning statement, a messaging framework.

              Matter Venture Partners: brand strategy and messaging with the founders. Everyday Dose: the 2025 positioning and messaging framework.
