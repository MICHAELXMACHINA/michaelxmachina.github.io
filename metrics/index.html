2021–2023: Building Scalable Brand and Messaging Foundations

Jack Savage launched Everyday Dose solo in Jan '21. When I joined in Sep '21, he had four junior freelancers supporting design, marketing, and customer service. Jack had led the development of a distinct and memorable visual brand, but lacked support for brand and content strategy, and messaging execution. So when he brought me in I:

  • established brand and messaging guidelines
  • developed a science-first voice that avoided over-claiming, while keeping the brand fun and accessible
  • led messaging for website, social media, products, packaging, and accessories
  • helped establish operational standards required for a rapidly growing small business to scale sustainably

When Jack pushed for the entire core team to be full-time employees, I decided to continue working across many different consulting projects, but left the door open for future collaboration.

2025: Improving Narrative Coherence Across Teams and Touchpoints

In May 2025, a few months before expanding from D2C into retail, Jack asked if I had capacity to help them with the transition. He said something like: "You know the brand better than anyone I could find since you helped build it, and I can't afford another temporary hire who doesn't get our voice and culture."

I returned in a near-full-time capacity to a much larger, more dynamic organization as Messaging Lead and Brand Strategist, where I:

  • audited, refined, and expanded brand positioning and messaging frameworks to align messaging across departments, in close collaboration with the executive team and Brand Strategy Lead
  • owned messaging across website, product, packaging, sponsorships, podcast ads, and events. (A dedicated Social Media Manager was handling social by this point.)
  • briefed various segments of UGC creators to help them more easily share grounded, research-backed content in their own voices, while reducing hyperbolic, inaccurate claims of Everyday Dose as a kind of magical cure-all. (Unfortunately it didn't always work, but I certainly tried.)
  • led messaging and contributed to brand strategy for the launch into 1,500 Target stores across the country, including a massive out-of-home campaign across NYC, spanning Penn Station, taxis, and street side kiosks


After the retail launch, I stayed on part-time to finish a ghostwriting project with Jack. The door remains open, but my focus has shifted to AI safety.

Process

Brand Strategy

Identity

I start with the company goals, executive interviews, research, and working sessions, then put a first strategy back in front of the team.

  • Purpose
  • Vision
  • Mission
  • Values

Positioning

Where the company belongs in its market, and why someone would choose it. I focus on the benefits for the people using the product, alongside the technical features.

  • Market Research
  • Market Overview
  • Competitive Analysis
  • Differentiators
  • Positioning Recommendation
  • Positioning Statement

Expression

How the brand sounds and tells its story. I brief the design team on the strategy and join reviews so the words and visuals stay aligned.

  • Naming (if needed)
  • Story
  • Personality
  • Voice
  • Manifesto
  • Tagline
  • Sample Messaging

Content

Content Strategy

I first map out the information architecture and sitemap to ensure that the overall structure will tell a coherent story. I then build out the structure and narrative beats of individual pages, beginning with the homepage, giving designers everything they need to build high-resolution wireframes. I also guide the creation of imagery and sometimes even storyboard and direct animations as pages develop.

Messaging

I write the headlines that introduce the story, and work with technical teams on the detail. I turn their input into clear language and flag inconsistencies or missing explanations.

On large and complex projects with Future Works, I often served as a connective node between team members, briefing and managing collaboration across leadership, marketing, design, and engineering. This led to offering internal operational support to Future Works as part of my Associate Strategy Director role.
