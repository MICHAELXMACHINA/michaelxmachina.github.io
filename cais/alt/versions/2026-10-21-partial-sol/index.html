2021–2023: Building scaleable brand and messaging foundations.

Jack launched Everyday Dose solo in Jan '21. When I joined in Sep '21, he had four junior freelancers supporting design, marketing, and customer service. Jack had led the development of a distinct and memorable visual brand, but lacked support for brand and content strategy, and messaging execution. So when he brought me in I: • established brand and messaging guidelines • developed a science-first voice that avoided over-claiming, while keeping the brand fun and accessible • led messaging for website, social media, products, packaging, and accessories • helped establish operational standards required for a rapidly growing small business to scale sustainably When Jack pushed for everyone on the core team to be full-time employees, I chose to continue working across many different consulting projects, but left the door open for future collaboration.

2025: Improving brand and messaging coherence to support expansion into retail.

When Jack fired his head copywriter in late May '25, just two months before expanding from D2C into retail, he asked if I had capacity to help them through the transition. "You know the brand better than anyone I could find since you helped build it, and I can't afford another hire who doesn't understand our voice and culture." I returned in a near-full-time capacity to a much larger, more dynamic organization as Messaging Lead and Brand Strategist, where I: • established and iteratively improved brand positioning and messaging frameworks to bring coherence across teams and touchpoints, in close collaboration with the executive team and Brand Strategy Lead • owned messaging across website, product, packaging, sponsorships, podcast ads, and events. (A dedicated Social Media Manager handled social posts.) • briefed various segments of UGC creators to help them more easily share grounded, research-backed content in their own voices to reduce hyperbolic and inaccurate claims of Everyday Dose as a kind of magical cure-all. (Unfortunately it didn't always work, but I certainly tried.) • led messaging and contributed to brand strategy for the launch into 1,500 Target stores across the country, including a massive out-of-home campaign across NYC, spanning Penn Station, taxis, and street side kiosks

    Process

    Brand Strategy

    Identity

    I start with the company goals, executive interviews, research, and working sessions, then put a first strategy back in front of the team.

    • Purpose
    • Vision
    • Mission
    • Values

    Positioning

    Where the company belongs in its market, and why someone would choose it.

    • Market Research
    • Market Overview
    • Competitive Analysis
    • Differentiators
    • Positioning Recommendation
    • Positioning Statement

    Expression

    How the brand sounds and tells its story. I brief the design team on the strategy and join reviews so the words and visuals stay aligned.

    • Naming (if needed)
    • Story
    • Personality
    • Voice
    • Manifesto
    • Tagline
    • Sample Messaging

    Content

    Content Strategy

    How I map the story across a website and other channels: the page order, narrative blocks, and what each audience needs to understand.

    Messaging

    The words that help people understand the product, care about it, and act.

    On complex projects, I connect technical teams, clients, writers and designers. I turn interviews into a shared plan, map pages and wireframes with the design team, and flag gaps before the final wording is written.
